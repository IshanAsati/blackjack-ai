How this mode works

The neural model chooses whether to bet one initial unit or observe. A fixed strategy plays hit, stand, and double. Another player keeps the shoe moving when we sit out. All cards become visible after the hand. We assume free observation, entry on any round, no bankroll cap, and no splitting or surrender. Gains and losses from doubling are included. Live intervals describe the session; repeatedly watching them does not replace a fixed-length test.
